How to Play - Sabor Termo

Guess the secret culinary term in 6 attempts!

Each guess must be a valid English word or expression from our gastronomic flavors and textures list.

Color Examples:
M
The letter M is in the word and in the correct spot.
I
The letter I is in the word, but in a different spot.
L
The letter L is not in the secret term.
Hints & Spaces:
  • Spaces and hyphens are automatically revealed in the grid.
  • You can ask for up to 2 hints per match using the lightbulb button.
  • You can inspect all available words by clicking the book icon in the top header.
